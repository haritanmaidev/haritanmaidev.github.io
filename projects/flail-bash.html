Project Overview

This is the prototype of a roguelike survival game where players are equipped with physics-based weapon and tools to survive waves of enemies and bosses in a cubic gravity-shifting arena. The gameplay revolves around players constantly maintaining momentum-based fluid movements to stack up force which scales the attack power of their physics-based weapons. Each face of the arena features different terrains which the players must learn to nagivate efficiently. Players can unlock new tools and weapons along with random power ups to progress further.
