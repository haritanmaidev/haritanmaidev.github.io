Project Overview

This is Mini Moog-inspired audio synthesizer, which was released in the 1970s, made with MetaSounds in Unreal Engine 5.

Unreal Engine Visual Scripting Metasound
Responsibilities
Audio Programming
Development Time
2.5 Weeks
Grade
Upper Second Class

Systems & Features

  • Subtractive Synthesis
  • Three Oscillator Waveforms: Sine, Saw, Triangle
  • MIDI Keyboard Inputs
  • Noise Generator
  • LFO Modulation
  • Bitcrusher Effect
  • ADSR EnvelopeControls
  • Pitch Bend and Glide Controls
  • VCA Controls
  • Blueprint to MetaSounds Communication

What I Learnt

Gained some knowledge and confidence regarding the implementation of audio synthesis in digital environments for creating or modifying audio within games.
