Project Overview

A level design project for Team Fortress 2's King of the Hill gamemode, made with Valve Hammer Editor. The aim was to gain experience in designing maps for multiplayer games by understanding how to balance and shape levels that provide a fair and fun experience for players, and how to incorporate feedback through iterations.

Level Design Valve Hammer Editor

What I Learnt

Gained insights on how level design affects the player's experience and how it can be used to provide various advantages and disadvantages to different classes of characters in a game. Additionally, I also learnt about the Team Fortress 2's level specifications such as the limits for scale and angles of terrains, objects, or structures within the game, as well as the importance of having playtesting and iterating on the design, based on peer feedback.
